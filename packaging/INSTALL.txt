24 SHIMMERS - install on macOS
==============================

Easiest:
  1. Right-click "install.command" and choose Open (then Open again in the
     warning). A plain double-click is blocked because the script isn't signed.
  2. Restart Ableton Live.

Or in Terminal, from this folder:
  bash install.command

By hand:
  - Copy "24 Shimmers.vst3"      to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  - Copy "24 Shimmers.component" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  - Then run:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"24 Shimmers.vst3"
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"24 Shimmers.component"

In Ableton Live:
  Settings > Plug-Ins: turn on "Use Audio Units v2" and/or
  "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", then press Rescan.
  The plugin appears in the browser under Plug-Ins > Thiel > 24 Shimmers.
